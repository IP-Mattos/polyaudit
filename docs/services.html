Before you copy that trader

Commissioned wallet audits

The free browser auditor gives you the headline numbers. A commissioned audit gives you the full forensic read: fee and rebate decomposition, maker/taker anatomy, luck shape, reconciliation against the venue's own figure — and an honest NOT RELIABLE when the books do not close, instead of a tidy wrong answer.

Every method is public in the repo. You are not paying for a secret — you are paying for the work, the cross-checks, and a written verdict you can hold against the record.

What you get

Three tiers, one standard

  • Single audit — $15

    One wallet, full public history walked to exhaustion. Real PnL (fees and both rebate programs netted), maker/taker split, concentration and halves, reconciliation verdict. Delivered as a written report within 24h.

  • Three wallets — $40

    Compare the wallets you are considering copying. Same standard, side-by-side table, one recommendation section: which economics replicate at your size and which need a tier you do not have.

  • Deep dive — $50

    One wallet, everything above plus era analysis (when the money was actually made), style-shift detection, and cross-checks against independent on-chain reconstruction where feasible.

Sample of the standard: the $8,000-bot case on the research page — a wallet with ~$100k of public profit whose trading lost money at the audit cutoff, reconciled to a $19 residual.

How to order

Two steps, no account

1. Email darkmasterofshadow@gmail.com with the wallet address(es) and the tier you want.

2. Pay in USDC on Polygon — the address comes with the order confirmation. Report lands in your inbox within 24 hours.

If a wallet cannot be reconstructed from public data, the report says NOT RELIABLE with the reason, and you pay half or nothing — your choice. Refusing to certify what cannot be verified is the product.

Audits are measurements of what happened, reconstructed from public APIs. They are not certified accounting, not predictions, and not financial advice.
