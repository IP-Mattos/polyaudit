Before you copy that trader

Commissioned wallet audits

The free browser auditor gives you the headline numbers. A commissioned audit gives you the full forensic read. Fee and rebate decomposition, maker/taker anatomy, luck shape, and a reconciliation against the venue's own figure. When the books do not close, you get an honest NOT RELIABLE instead of a tidy wrong answer.

Every method is public in the repo, so you are not paying for a secret. You are paying for the work, the cross-checks, and a written verdict you can hold against the record.

    One wallet, full public history walked to exhaustion. You get the real PnL with fees and both rebate programs netted in, the maker/taker split, the concentration and halves, and the reconciliation verdict. Delivered as a written report within 24h.

  • Three wallets · $40

    Compare the wallets you are thinking of copying. Same standard, a side-by-side table, and one recommendation section that says which economics replicate at your size and which ones need a tier you do not have.

How to order

Two steps, no account

1. Email darkmasterofshadow@gmail.com with the wallet address(es) and the tier you want.

2. Pay in USDC on Polygon. The address comes with the order confirmation, and the report lands in your inbox within 24 hours.

Audits are measurements of what happened, reconstructed from public APIs. They are not certified accounting, not predictions, and not financial advice.
