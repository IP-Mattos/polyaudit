Audit a wallet

Paste any Polymarket wallet address. The auditor walks its full public activity backwards, re-prices every fill, and nets fees and rebates into one real PnL. Then it checks the result against the identity official ≈ real + fees − rebates. If the books do not tie out within 5%, it says NOT RELIABLE instead of printing a tidy wrong answer.

Try the exhibit wallet, 0xf418d3a1a941292f9c8707d62a14980c5beb95a3. It is the one that sells its bot for $8,000.

Large wallets with hundreds of thousands of events take several minutes. Requests are spaced about 350ms and walked 500 events at a time, out of respect for a public API. Everything runs in your browser. No server, no account, no API key, and every request is a plain GET to Polymarket's public endpoints. If you cancel, it reports the partial period honestly and flags it as partial.

This measures what happened. It is not advice. Some wallets cannot be reconstructed from public activity alone, and when the reconciliation guard fails, the right output is to refuse.
